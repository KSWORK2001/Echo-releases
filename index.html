Meeting mode

It listens the whole time. It speaks when you ask.

Transcription runs continuously so nothing is missed, but the model is never called on its own. It answers on a quick action, with the live transcript attached, so nothing leaves your machine because a timer went off.

  • Two streams, kept apart. System audio and your microphone are captured separately, so the transcript knows the difference between you and the room.
  • It stops when you start. Begin speaking mid-answer and the reply halts rather than talking over you.
  • The call ends with a list. Action items and ticket drafts are pulled from the transcript, ready to edit or push to GitHub and Jira.

Focus mode

A task list that never takes the screen.

The bar is replaced by a translucent panel pinned to the corner of your primary display. You can run the whole thing without reaching for the mouse, and dim it until it nearly disappears.

Ping

Hand it the mouse. Take it back whenever.

Describe a routine in a sentence. Echo screenshots the desktop, decides on one action, performs it, then checks the screen actually changed before deciding again. Every step shows its reasoning as it happens.

  1. 1 Look

    Captures the screen and marks where the cursor really is.

  2. 2 Decide

    Returns exactly one action, and the reason for it.

  3. 3 Act

    Clicks the element itself when the app exposes one.

  4. 4 Verify

    Confirms the screen moved, or says so and changes route.

It gives the cursor back

A physical click reads where your pointer was and puts it straight back, about a tenth of a millisecond later. Your desktop does not end up somewhere you did not leave it.

It yields to you

Touch your own keyboard or mouse and the run pauses before its next action, then picks up once you have stopped. Pause long enough and it takes a fresh screenshot rather than acting on a stale one.

It stops dead

CtrlAltEsc ends any run instantly, on Windows and macOS, whatever it is in the middle of.

Chain the routines together

Drag from one routine to the next and the second fires when the first finishes, on success, on failure, or either way. Drop in a wait, a message, or a hold until a set time. Everything queues through one lane, because there is only one desktop.

Your machine

The parts that would normally cost you a subscription run locally.

Speech On device. A 0.6B-parameter speech model runs on your own machine, so a recording does not have to leave it to become a transcript.
AI Your account. Replies go through your own Claude subscription, or any provider you point it at with your own key. There is no meter in between.
History A file you own. Conversations, tasks and routines live in a local database on your disk.
Keys System keychain. Tokens and API keys sit where your browser keeps its passwords, not in app storage.
Capture Invisible to it. Echo's windows are hidden from screen capture, so sharing your screen does not share Echo.
